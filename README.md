NIVA 🌸

AI-Powered Menstrual Wellness & Cycle Companion

NIVA is a modern, privacy-focused menstrual wellness platform designed to help women understand and manage their menstrual cycles through intelligent tracking, personalized insights, AI-powered assistance, and wellness tools.

NIVA goes beyond basic period tracking by bringing cycle tracking, symptom monitoring, mood tracking, wellness insights, education, reminders, and conversational AI into one simple and professional platform.

🤖 NIVA AI

NIVA includes an AI-powered conversational wellness assistant that can help users understand their cycle and general wellness information.

Example questions:

«"Why do I feel tired around my period?"»

«"Explain my current cycle phase."»

«"What patterns do you see in my symptoms?"»

«"What can I do for general period discomfort?"»

The AI can use voluntarily provided cycle information to create more personalized responses.

Important: NIVA AI provides general wellness information and is not a replacement for professional medical advice or diagnosis.

🛡️ Privacy & Safety

NIVA is designed around the principle that menstrual and wellness information is sensitive personal data.

The platform aims to:

- Minimize unnecessary data collection
- Keep user information isolated
- Protect authentication credentials
- Secure API communication
- Avoid exposing sensitive information publicly
- Give users control over their data
- Clearly distinguish AI-generated information from verified data

NIVA is a wellness platform and does not diagnose medical conditions or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

---

🚫 What NIVA Does Not Include

NIVA is focused on digital menstrual wellness.

It intentionally does not include:

- Product marketplace
- Online shopping
- Sanitary-product purchasing
- Shopping cart
- Checkout
